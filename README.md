# Canva Element Grabber & Inserter (Chrome Extension)

A Chrome Extension (Manifest V3) that lets you inspect, extract, and drop any graphic or element from Canva's sidebar directly onto your canvas page as an external image/vector upload.

---

## ✨ Features

- **🎯 Inspector Mode (`Alt + G`)**: Hover over any graphic, sticker, or photo in Canva's Elements or Templates sidebar. It highlights the graphic and lets you grab it with a single click.
- **⚡ Instant Auto-Drop**: Automatically converts the grabbed graphic into a native image file and drops/pastes it directly into your active Canva canvas page.
- **📦 Floating Draggable Tray**: Keep a tray of all your captured elements right on the Canva screen. You can **drag and drop** them onto the canvas with your mouse at any time!
- **💎 High-Res Clean Extraction**: Automatically cleans thumbnail query limits (like `w=150`, `h=150`) to request the highest resolution PNGs and pristine SVG vectors.
- **💾 One-Click Download**: Download any extracted vector SVG or PNG directly to your computer.
- **🚀 No DevTools Required**: Everything works directly on the Canva interface without needing to manually inspect elements or copy URLs.

---

## 🛠️ How to Install in Google Chrome

1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Navigate to `chrome://extensions/` in the address bar.
3. Turn ON **Developer mode** (toggle switch in the top-right corner).
4. Click the **"Load unpacked"** button in the top-left corner.
5. Select this project folder:
   ```
   T:\PROJECT\canva\canva-element-grabber
   ```
6. The extension **Canva Element Grabber & Inserter** is now installed and active!

---

## 🚀 How to Use It on Canva

1. Open any design in [Canva](https://www.canva.com/design).
2. You will see the sleek **"Canva Grabber"** floating toolbar at the bottom-right corner of your screen.
3. Click the **🎯 Inspect** button (or press `Alt + G`).
4. Move your cursor over any graphic or element in the Canva Elements sidebar. You'll see a purple highlight box.
5. **Click the graphic**:
   - The extension extracts the direct high-res image/SVG.
   - If **Auto-Drop** is ON (default), it immediately places it onto your active design canvas!
   - It also saves it into your **Tray**.
6. **Want to drag it yourself?**
   - Click the **Tray** button to open the drawer.
   - Simply click and drag any thumbnail from the tray directly onto your canvas with your mouse!
